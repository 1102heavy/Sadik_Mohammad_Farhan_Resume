System Architecture Controls & Safety Dashboard & Telemetry

  • Own LV system architecture and integration with the high-voltage powertrain, GLV BMS and ECU, ensuring compliance with FSAE EV rules.
  • Driving a redesign of the dashboard and live telemetry system from a Teensy 4.1 dev board to a custom STM32-based controller. Key Engineering Projects

Final Year Project – CM200DX & GLV Battery Management

2025 – Present
Cascadia CM200DX · GLV BMS · Path to Accumulator BMS
  • Final Year Project combining high-voltage powertrain and low-voltage safety: Cascadia System Integration – HV & LV and a new GLV Battery Management System for the NUS FSAE EV.
  • Integrated the Cascadia CM200DX inverter with the Emrax motor, accumulator, ECU and LV systems, including precharge, contactor logic, torque request handling and diagnostics, with a focus on FSAE EV safety rules.
  • Analysed MoTeC and inverter logs to compare the CM200DX against the previous Bamocar D3 setup: the Cascadia package delivers more consistent torque at the 80 kW power limit, reduces time spent in thermal derating and improves repeatability of acceleration over longer runs.
  • Documented improvements in drivability from the CM200DX’s current control and torque shaping – particularly in launch and mid-corner exit – and linked these to calibration choices for torque limits and regen.
  • Designed a GLV BMS architecture from first principles: sensing, protection, communication and software strategy for the grounded low-voltage pack that supplies control electronics and ECUs.
  • Planned the GLV BMS so that its firmware and communication concepts can be extended into a future accumulator BMS using galvanically-isolated slave boards (Infineon TLE9012/TLE9015 with iso-UART daisy-chaining).
